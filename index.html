Load the briefing package (PDF) or paste its text. It is read on this device only: nothing is uploaded, and it works offline. The app reads the coordinates list, the nav log (ELTM and FL) and the header times.

Lat/Lon as N0244.6 / E10141.9 or decimal. Airports from the built-in list fill in automatically. ETA (HHMM UTC) is optional on waypoints; without it the time is spread by distance. Times roll past 0000Z automatically. FL is the cruise level from that fix on (blank = same as before). Changing an FL changes the rest of that level segment; changing an ETA moves every later ETA. Both recalculate straight away.

How to read the globe
Coloured lines show where the sun is at each prayer's angle at the slider time, for the aircraft's current altitude (ihtiyat is added to the time, not drawn). As the slider moves, watch the aircraft cross them. Shading: day, then twilight (to 18°), then night. The sun and moon sit over the point where each is overhead; the moon is drawn in its current phase, lit side towards the sun. An arrow on the edge means it is out of view in that direction. Drag to rotate; pinch, scroll or double-tap to zoom. Tap a prayer above to jump to the moment the aircraft crosses its line: the crossing is where the line meets the aircraft, not where it meets the route at other times.

Qibla is given as a clock position from the aircraft's nose (12 o'clock = straight ahead, 3 = right, 9 = left), from the great-circle bearing to the Kaaba and the planned track. Drift is not included: with a crosswind the nose points a few degrees off the track. QDM and track are magnetic (World Magnetic Model 2025). Prayer-window opening times are rounded up to the minute and closing times are rounded down. This is a reference prototype: check it against your authority before relying on it.
